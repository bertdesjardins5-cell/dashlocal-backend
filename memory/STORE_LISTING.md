## Full description (Google Play max 4000 / App Store max 4000)
LocalDash is same-day local delivery for neighbourhood stores, pharmacies and offices, with live tracking for customers and simple tools for drivers.

FOR CUSTOMERS
• Live tracking: watch your driver on the map with an arrival time that updates as they go
• Text alerts when your order is on the way and when it's delivered
• Photo or signature proof of delivery for every drop
• Leave a gate code or drop-off note for your driver
• Pay securely by card in the app when your store asks for payment (processed by Square)
• Rate your delivery with stars and a comment so stores know how it went
• Call or text your driver without sharing your phone number

FOR STORES
• Enter a delivery in seconds, or upload a spreadsheet for many
• Addresses are checked on the map automatically
• Priority deliveries and delivery time windows
• Email alerts when a drop runs late or fails
• Weekly reports, invoices and receipts in Canadian dollars with HST
• See customer ratings and comments for every delivery, by driver
• Saved card on file for easy weekly billing

FOR DRIVERS
• Routes put in the best order automatically
• Turn-by-turn navigation in one tap
• Scan parcel labels, take a proof photo or collect a signature
• Stops marked arrived automatically when you reach the address
• Contact customers through masked calls and texts
• See your ratings, compliments and weekly scorecard

PRIVACY FIRST
Phone numbers stay private with masked calling and texting. Driver location is only used during an active delivery route. Customers and stores are never tracked. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

LocalDash serves local businesses and their customers in Canada.
Questions? Visit https://localdash.ca
